Quantinuum’s first InQuanto Summer School, supported by the UK’s National Quantum Computing Centre (NQCC), combined lectures, hands-on workshops, and mini-projects to help participants connect fundamental concepts with practical research. By the end of the week, participants were applying those ideas to problems ranging from molecular electronic structure to quantum state-preparation circuits.
