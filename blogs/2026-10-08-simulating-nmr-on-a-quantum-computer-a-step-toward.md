In our latest work, we demonstrate the most accurate large-scale digital NMR simulation performed on quantum hardware to date. Using Quantinuum's System Model H2, we carried out an end-to-end simulation of a classically challenging NMR experiment, reproducing key spectral features that previous hardware demonstrations were unable to capture.
